Our Services

At Abo Ameer Garage for Vehicle Maintenance, we provide professional maintenance and diagnostic services for cars, trucks, and buses. Using modern diagnostic equipment and experienced technicians, we ensure every vehicle is serviced accurately, efficiently, and safely.

Electrical System Repair

We diagnose and repair all types of automotive electrical problems, including wiring faults, lighting systems, batteries, charging systems, starters, alternators, and electronic control components.

Computer Diagnostics

Our garage is equipped with modern diagnostic scanners that communicate directly with your vehicle's ECUs.

Air System Services

We specialize in diagnosing and repairing air-related systems, especially for trucks and buses equipped with air brakes and pneumatic systems.

ABS (Anti-lock Braking System)

Our technicians perform complete ABS diagnostics and repairs, including wheel speed sensors, ABS modules, hydraulic units, and electronic control systems.

Periodic Maintenance

Regular maintenance is essential for extending vehicle life and preventing unexpected breakdowns.
