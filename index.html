Our Services

At Abo Ameer Garage for Vehicle Maintenance, we provide professional maintenance and diagnostic services for cars, trucks, and buses. Using modern diagnostic equipment and experienced technicians, we ensure every vehicle is serviced accurately, efficiently, and safely.

Electrical System Repair

We diagnose and repair all types of automotive electrical problems, including wiring faults, lighting systems, batteries, charging systems, starters, alternators, and electronic control components. Our advanced testing equipment allows us to identify electrical issues quickly and accurately.

Computer Diagnostics

Our garage is equipped with modern diagnostic scanners that communicate directly with your vehicle's electronic control units (ECUs). We can detect fault codes, monitor live sensor data, troubleshoot engine and transmission problems, reset warning lights, and perform system programming when required.

Air System Services

We specialize in diagnosing and repairing air-related systems, especially for trucks and buses equipped with air brakes and pneumatic systems. We inspect air compressors, valves, hoses, air tanks, and suspension components to ensure reliable and safe operation.

ABS (Anti-lock Braking System)

Our technicians perform complete ABS diagnostics and repairs, including wheel speed sensors, ABS modules, hydraulic units, and electronic control systems. A properly functioning ABS system improves braking performance and vehicle safety under all driving conditions.

Periodic Maintenance

Regular maintenance is essential for extending vehicle life and preventing unexpected breakdowns. Our periodic service includes oil and filter replacement, fluid checks, brake inspection, battery testing, suspension inspection, tire condition assessment, and a complete vehicle health check.
